The non-compete constraint. DEC's engineers were subject to Massachusetts non-compete law from day one. When Edson de Castro left to found Data General, DEC sued immediately for trade secret misappropriation — illustrating exactly the legal environment Massachusetts created. The litigation consumed resources and sent a signal to every subsequent DEC engineer who contemplated the same move. In California, the same departure would have faced no legal barrier.

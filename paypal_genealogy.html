The PayPal Mafia

Founded in 1998 as X.com (merging with Confinity to become PayPal), the company pioneered digital payments and attracted an exceptional concentration of entrepreneurial talent. PayPal alumni founded or funded Stripe, Square, LinkedIn, SpaceX, Tesla, Slack, Notion, Palantir, and dozens more.

California's departure advantage

Operating in California with its unenforceable non-compete law, PayPal executives celebrated departures rather than litigating them. This culture of blessing departures created the conditions for some of the world's highest-impact startups.

The network effect. PayPal's genealogy demonstrates how California's legal environment doesn't just permit founder departures — it encourages them. The "PayPal Mafia" is perhaps the most pronounced example of how a non-compete-free jurisdiction produces a compounding explosion of innovation and capital.

The PayPal Mafia

Founded in 1998 as X.com (merging with Confinity to become PayPal), the company pioneered digital payments and attracted an exceptional concentration of entrepreneurial talent. PayPal alumni went on to found or lead Tesla, SpaceX, LinkedIn, YouTube, Square, Stripe, Yelp, and dozens of other major companies.

California's departure advantage

Operating in California with its unenforceable non-compete law, PayPal executives celebrated departures rather than litigating them. This culture of blessing departures created the conditions for the most prolific founder network in modern tech history.

The network effect. PayPal's genealogy demonstrates how California's legal environment doesn't just permit founder departures — it encourages them. The "PayPal Mafia" is perhaps the clearest case study in how non-compete law directly shapes entrepreneurial ecosystems and generational wealth creation.

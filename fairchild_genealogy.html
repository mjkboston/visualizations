The California difference. Every departure on this tree — from Shockley to Fairchild, from Fairchild to Intel, from Intel alumni to Apple — was legally uninhibited by California's Business & Professions Code §16600. In Massachusetts, each move would have required navigating a non-compete agreement. The departures that built this $2 trillion lineage were not exceptional courage; they were ordinary ambition, freed by law.

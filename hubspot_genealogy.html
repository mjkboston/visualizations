The HubSpot story

Founded in 2006 by Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah in Cambridge, Massachusetts. HubSpot pioneered inbound marketing software and became a market leader in SaaS. As a Massachusetts company, HubSpot's growth trajectory was shaped by the region's non-compete culture.

Talent and departures

Unlike Silicon Valley, Massachusetts non-compete agreements constrained departures from established companies. HubSpot's genealogy reflects both the talent it attracted and the limitations on spin-outs that Massachusetts law created.

The Massachusetts context. HubSpot's success occurred in an era when Massachusetts still enforced relatively restrictive non-compete agreements. This visualization explores how that legal environment shaped HubSpot's talent strategy and competitive landscape.

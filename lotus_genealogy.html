Lotus 1-2-3 and its era

Founded in 1982 by Mitchell Kapor in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Lotus 1-2-3 became the dominant spreadsheet application of the 1980s and 1990s. The company attracted elite engineers and entrepreneurs who would shape the software industry.

Constrained spin-outs, lasting influence

Unlike the Fairchild genealogy in California, Lotus spinoffs in Massachusetts faced legal headwinds from non-compete agreements. Yet the company's talent pool and market innovations influenced generations of software entrepreneurs and ventures.

The Route 128 story. Lotus was a centerpiece of Route 128's competitive ecosystem, competing directly with Silicon Valley giants like Excel. This genealogy explores how Massachusetts' non-compete enforcement shaped both Lotus' internal loyalty and the constrained departure culture that followed.
